Chris Pratt said he would love to reunite with James Gunn for a fourth Guardians of the Galaxy film, just days after Gunn and co‑chief Peter Safran were removed from DC Studios. The announcement came amid a leadership shake‑up at DC, where Jeremy Latcham is set to take over as president and chief creative officer. Pratt’s enthusiasm highlights how the Marvel Cinematic Universe could benefit from Gunn’s return, while also underscoring the volatility of studio politics.

<Callout type="takeaway">The prospect of Gunn directing Guardians 4 could reshape the MCU’s future after his DC departure.</Callout>

## What happened
On November 9, Warner Bros. Discovery announced that James Gunn and Peter Safran were being ousted from their co‑chairman roles at DC Studios following the Skydance merger. Jeremy Latcham was named the incoming president and chief creative officer, effective December 2026.

In an interview with Moviezine, Chris Pratt expressed that working with Gunn again would be “awesome,” noting that Gunn directed the three previous Guardians films and that the franchise’s end‑credits of Vol. 3 hinted at Star‑Lord’s return. Pratt, who has portrayed Peter Quill in six MCU entries, said he has not yet spoken to Gunn since the DC news broke.

## Why it matters
Gunn’s departure from DC removes a proven franchise builder from a studio that has struggled to match the MCU’s box‑office dominance, potentially freeing him to focus on Marvel projects. For Marvel, securing Gunn could reinforce the tonal consistency of the Guardians saga and sustain audience interest beyond Vol. 3. For fans, the prospect of a fourth film offers narrative closure for Star‑Lord, whose story was left open-ended.
