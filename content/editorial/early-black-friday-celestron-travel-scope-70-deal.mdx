## How to think about it
Treat the Travel Scope 70 as a stepping‑stone instrument. Start by learning to locate the Moon and bright planets, then experiment with simple star clusters. Pair the telescope with a star‑chart app to reduce the learning curve, and consider adding a third eyepiece or a low‑cost Barlow lens for modest magnification upgrades. If you find yourself craving deeper‑sky targets, plan for a larger aperture upgrade later rather than expecting this model to replace a mid‑range telescope.

<Callout type="warning">Do not expect the 70 mm scope to resolve faint nebulae or galaxies; it excels at bright objects and educational use only.</Callout>
