A viral post on social media claimed that 28‑year‑old actor Jaden Smith is dating—or even engaged to—82‑year‑old French fashion figure Michele Lamy. The claim circulated with edited photos and videos that appeared to show the two together. Fact‑checking outlets have found no evidence of any relationship, noting that the images are fabricated and that Lamy is married to designer Rick Owens. The story illustrates how quickly false narratives can gain traction online. Understanding why this rumor spread matters for anyone navigating digital information.

<Callout type="takeaway">The Jaden Smith‑Michele Lamy rumor is completely unfounded, underscoring the need for critical scrutiny of viral claims.</Callout>

## What happened
The claim originated on platforms such as TikTok and Twitter, where users shared short clips suggesting a romantic link between Jaden Smith, 28, and Michele Lamy, 82. The posts referenced alleged photos and videos of the pair kissing, presenting them as proof of an engagement.

Fact‑checking organizations examined the evidence and confirmed that the media were digitally altered. Lamy has been publicly married to designer Rick Owens for years, and no reputable news outlet has reported any relationship with Smith. The fabricated content was quickly labeled false, and the rumor was dismissed as a hoax.

## Why it matters
Misinformation of this sort erodes public trust in both celebrities and the media, making it harder for genuine stories to be believed. It also weaponizes personal reputations, potentially harming the individuals involved and their families. The rapid spread of such rumors highlights gaps in digital literacy, showing how easily manipulated visuals can bypass critical thinking.

## How to think about it
Treat any sensational claim with a three‑step filter: (1) check the source—does a reputable outlet report it? (2) examine the media—look for signs of editing or context loss. (3) cross‑reference with fact‑checking sites before sharing. Building this habit reduces the chance of amplifying false narratives and strengthens collective digital resilience.

<Callout type="warning">Do not repost or forward unverified images or videos, even if they appear convincing; always seek confirmation from trusted fact‑checkers first.</Callout>
