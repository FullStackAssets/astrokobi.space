MercadoLibre’s shares plunged 38% in November, a stark contrast to the steady performance of SpaceX’s private valuation and the modest gains of the so‑called Magnificent Seven. The drop sparked debate among investors about whether the e‑commerce giant has become a bargain relative to high‑growth tech names. Understanding the drivers behind the slide and the comparative risk profiles is crucial for anyone shaping a portfolio for the next market cycle.

<Callout type="takeaway">At current levels, MercadoLibre offers a higher upside potential but also greater downside risk than SpaceX and the Magnificent Seven.</Callout>

## What happened
In early November 2026, MercadoLibre (MELI) closed at a price that represented a 38% decline from its peak two months earlier, erasing billions of dollars in market cap after a series of earnings misses and a slowdown in Latin American e‑commerce growth.

Meanwhile, SpaceX, still privately held, maintained its latest valuation of roughly $150 billion, buoyed by continued launch contracts, while the publicly traded Magnificent Seven—Apple, Microsoft, Alphabet, Amazon, Meta, Nvidia and Tesla—recorded modest double‑digit gains on earnings beats and AI‑driven revenue growth.

## Why it matters
The contrast highlights a classic risk‑return trade‑off: MercadoLibre’s price drop may reflect both genuine operational challenges and an over‑reaction that creates buying opportunities. By contrast, SpaceX’s valuation is insulated from public market volatility but remains illiquid, while the Magnificent Seven benefit from strong cash flows and investor confidence but trade at premium multiples. Investors must weigh growth prospects, liquidity, and valuation gaps when allocating capital across these disparate assets.

## How to think about it
Treat the decision as a portfolio allocation problem: assign a small, risk‑adjusted slice to MercadoLibre if you believe the market has over‑punished the stock, keep the bulk in liquid, high‑quality tech names for stability, and consider a separate private‑equity exposure or fund if you want participation in SpaceX’s growth without direct ownership.

<Callout type="warning">Do not allocate a large portion of a diversified portfolio to MercadoLibre solely on the basis of its price decline; the underlying business challenges remain significant.</Callout>
