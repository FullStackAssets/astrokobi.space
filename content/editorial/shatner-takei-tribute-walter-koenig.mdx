On October 25, 2026, the Star Trek community mourned the loss of Walter Koenig, the actor best known for playing Lt. Pavel Chekov. The news arrived during the franchise’s 60th‑anniversary celebrations, which also marked the recent passing of Patrick Stewart. Two of the remaining original series crew members, William Shatner (age 95) and George Takei (age 89), posted personal tributes on Instagram. Their messages highlighted Koenig’s dedication to fans and his role as a friend beyond the set. The outpouring of respect illustrates how the series’ legacy continues to resonate across generations.

<Callout type="takeaway">The heartfelt tributes underscore Koenig’s lasting influence on both the Star Trek universe and its fans.</Callout>

## What happened
Walter Koenig died at age 90 on Sunday, Oct. 25, 2026, prompting an immediate wave of condolences from fellow cast members and fans alike.

William Shatner shared on Instagram, "I am deeply saddened to learn of Walter Koenig’s passing. Although we did not know each other well away from the set, we shared the later seasons of the original 'Star Trek' series and the six films that followed. What I observed, and what so many fans have expressed over the years, was a man who genuinely loved the 'Star Trek' community. At conventions, Walter was rarely content to remain behind a signing table. He preferred to be among the people who had kept 'Star Trek' alive for so many decades. That kind of genuine connection is rare, and it will be remembered by everyone who came into contact with him. My condolences to his family, his friends, and the countless fans whose lives he touched."

George Takei posted, "My heart is heavy. Walter Koenig, our Ensign Pavel Chekov, passed away early today. Walter and I met on the bridge of the Enterprise in the 1960s, and we stayed friends for nearly six decades. When Brad and I married in 2008, Walter stood beside us as our best man. Just last month, on Sept. 14, he turned 90, on the same day Brad and I celebrated our 18th wedding anniversary. I will always treasure that he and I shared that date. Walter was more than Chekov. He was a writer, a teacher, a thoughtful man, and a loyal friend. To the fans who loved him for so many years,".
