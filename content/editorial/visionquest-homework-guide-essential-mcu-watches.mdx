Disney+ has launched VisionQuest, the long‑awaited continuation of the Vision saga that began with 2021’s WandaVision. The first four episodes explore a fractured Vision—White Vision, Hex Vision, and a new Ultron‑inspired construct—while teasing the conclusion of the trilogy announced by Marvel Studios. For anyone who wants to follow the story without getting lost in decades of MCU history, knowing which movies and shows precede VisionQuest is essential. This guide distills the sprawling backstory into a short watch list so viewers can dive straight into the new series. Skipping the prerequisite material risks missing key character motivations and plot twists.

<Callout type="takeaway">Understanding VisionQuest requires watching a focused set of MCU titles that establish Vision’s death, resurrection, and multiversal entanglements.</Callout>

## What happened
VisionQuest premiered on Disney+ with four tightly plotted episodes that reunite Paul Bettany’s Vision in three distinct forms: the original synthetic being, a hex‑infused version born from Wanda’s grief, and an emergent Ultron‑like entity. The series is billed as the final chapter of the “Vision trilogy,” following the 2021 Disney+ series WandaVision and the 2024 limited series Agatha All Along, both of which expanded the magical and multiversal stakes surrounding the character.

The narrative builds on key MCU moments: Infinity War (2018) where Thanos removed Vision’s Mind Stone, killing him; WandaVision’s reconstruction of a new Vision using salvaged parts; and the subsequent magical fallout explored in Agatha All Along. By weaving these events together, VisionQuest positions itself as both a resolution and a springboard for future MCU storylines.

To prepare, Marvel recommends a concise watch list: Avengers: Infinity War, WandaVision (all episodes), Agatha All Along, and optionally Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness for deeper context on Wanda’s arc. This curated set captures the essential plot threads without demanding a full marathon of every MCU title.

## Why it matters
VisionQuest is more than another superhero spin‑off; it ties together narrative threads that have defined the MCU’s exploration of grief, identity, and reality manipulation. For casual viewers, the guide prevents confusion and preserves emotional impact, while for hardcore fans it highlights how Marvel is using Vision’s multiple incarnations to explore philosophical questions about consciousness. The series also signals Marvel’s strategy of building tightly linked story arcs across film and streaming platforms, influencing how future phases will be consumed.

## How to think about it
Treat the watch list as a narrative scaffold: start with Infinity War to understand Vision’s original demise, then move to WandaVision to see his reconstruction, followed by Agatha All Along for the magical consequences that shape the multiverse. If you have extra time, a quick rewatch of Doctor Strange in the Multiverse of Madness can deepen your grasp of Wanda’s evolving powers. Approach VisionQuest with these anchors in mind, and let the series fill in the gaps rather than trying to recall every MCU cameo.

<Callout type="warning">Do not binge‑watch the entire MCU before VisionQuest; focus on the listed titles to avoid burnout and unnecessary spoilers.</Callout>
